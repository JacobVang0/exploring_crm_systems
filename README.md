## Executive Summary

This project explores Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems using Generative AI tools. The research examines CRM concepts, compares commercial and open-source CRM products, evaluates an open-source CRM solution, analyzes CRM architecture, and reflects on the effectiveness of AI-assisted software research.

## AI Tools Used

- ChatGPT

## CRM Research Findings

CRM systems help organizations manage customer relationships, improve sales processes, support marketing activities, and provide reporting and analytics capabilities.

## CRM Product Comparisons

Commercial CRM systems researched:

- Salesforce
- HubSpot CRM
- Zoho CRM
- Microsoft Dynamics 365

Open-source CRM systems researched:

- SuiteCRM
- EspoCRM
- Odoo CRM
- Vtiger CRM

## Open Source CRM Evaluation

SuiteCRM was selected for evaluation because it is one of the most mature and widely used open-source CRM solutions.
