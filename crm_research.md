# CRM Research

## What is CRM?

Customer Relationship Management (CRM) is software used by organizations to manage interactions with current and potential customers. CRM systems centralize customer information, improve communication, and help businesses increase sales and customer satisfaction.

### Purpose

- Manage customer relationships
- Improve sales performance
- Track customer interactions
- Improve customer support
- Generate business insights

### History

CRM systems began in the 1980s as contact management software. During the 1990s, CRM evolved into sales force automation tools. Today, CRM platforms include sales, marketing, customer service, analytics, and AI-powered features.

### Evolution

- Contact Management
- Sales Force Automation
- Web-Based CRM
- Cloud CRM
- AI-Powered CRM

## Why Organizations Use CRM Systems

### Sales Management

CRM systems help sales teams track leads, opportunities, and customer interactions.

### Customer Management

Organizations maintain detailed customer records and communication history.

### Marketing

CRM systems help manage campaigns and customer segmentation.

### Customer Support

Support teams can track customer issues and service requests.

### Reporting and Analytics

Managers use dashboards and reports to monitor business performance.

## Business Problems Solved

### Retail

Tracks customer purchases and marketing campaigns.

### Healthcare

Manages patient relationships and communication.

### Education

Tracks student recruitment and engagement.

### Manufacturing

Manages customer accounts and sales opportunities.

### Nonprofits

Tracks donors, fundraising campaigns, and volunteer activities.
