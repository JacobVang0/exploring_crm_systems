# Prompts Used

## Prompt 1
What is a CRM system? Explain its purpose, history, and evolution.

## Prompt 2
Compare Salesforce, HubSpot CRM, Zoho CRM, and Microsoft Dynamics 365.

## Prompt 3
Compare SuiteCRM, EspoCRM, Odoo CRM, and Vtiger CRM.

## Prompt 4
Design a CRM system using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, PHP, and MySQL.

## Prompt 5
What are the strengths and weaknesses of AI-assisted software research?

# Reflection

## What AI Did Well

AI quickly summarized CRM concepts and compared software products.

## What AI Struggled With

Pricing information and product features sometimes required verification.

## What Required Validation

Product pricing, market share, and feature availability.

## Would I Trust AI?

AI is useful for research and learning, but important information should always be verified using official sources.
